Welcome to GHAZAL

GHAZAL (غزل) is a restaurant at KM 12, Rua Direita de Viana, Luanda. The kitchen cooks chaat, Sindhi home cooking, North and South Indian dishes, pizza and sweets, and the bar serves cocktails and mocktails.

Every dish carries a poetic name, in keeping with the word ghazal itself: a taste of poetry in every bite. We are open every day from 9:00 to 23:00.
